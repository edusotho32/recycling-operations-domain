# Provenance

The historical source inspected was `Atividade-de-DW`, whose local Git remote points to `edusotho32/Atividade-de-DW` and whose two commits are authored by `edusotho32`. The original artifact is a single-file React/Babel application for selective-collection operations, accompanied by a requirements PDF and an E2E script whose declared Puppeteer dependency was missing from `package.json`.

This repository is a new October 2026 extraction of the deterministic business rules. It does not copy the historical UI, seed people, CPF/CNPJ-like fixtures, screenshots or the requirements PDF. It makes no authorship claim over the attached academic requirements document. The domain rules were rewritten as pure TypeScript functions and covered with Bun tests.
